Diagnostic hypotheses and next investigations

Mean CPU for selected process set, CPU interval coverage

The selected processes may be waiting, running a serial phase, or covering only part of the job.

Suggested investigation Confirm the worker PID set and align CPU intervals with timestamped solver phases.

Minimum sampled system available memory fraction

The workstation may have experienced memory pressure during the capture.

Suggested investigation Check paging and other system activity in a controlled repeat.

Observed reductions after unsuccessful attempts

Repeated time-increment reductions may contribute additional solver work.

Suggested investigation Inspect the referenced increments and relevant convergence diagnostics.

Measurement and sharing limitations

  • Unclassified lines may contain unsupported diagnostics or ordinary output.
  • The capture may cover only part of the selected processes' lifetimes.
  • Selected processes and files may cover only part of the job. Linkage to one specific run is operator-supplied.
  • Physical disk and available memory counters describe the workstation.
  • Selected process transfer counters include non-disk I/O. Working-set sums may count shared pages more than once.
  • A performance change requires a controlled comparison. Numerical equivalence and physical validation require separate checks.

Review the report and its measurements before external sharing.
